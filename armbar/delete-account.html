Delete your ARMBAR account

ARMBAR: Arm Wrestling Career Simulator, published by sLumLake (Ali Dinch).

You only have an account if you signed in with Google or an email address. Guests have no account; their careers are stored on the phone only and are removed by uninstalling the game.

How to delete it

  1. Open ARMBAR and go to Store.
  2. Open About & credits.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name and user id.
  • Your cloud saves: every career backed up to the account.

What is kept

  • Purchase records held by Google Play and by our purchase provider RevenueCat, for as long as needed to meet tax and accounting obligations. These are linked to a user id, not to your name.

Deleting your account does not refund or cancel purchases. See the Privacy Policy for details.
